For payment through SBP, the payment method is saved with the User’s express consent.

This section applies to a trial period only in the “Trial period with subscription” mode (clause 4.5). For a one-off trial period, the payment method is not saved and no automatic charge is made.

4.A.2. Frequency, amount, and timing of charges. An automatic charge is made once per billing period in the amount of the full price of the User’s current paid Plan. A billing period is 30 (thirty) calendar days unless otherwise specified for the Plan; the first billing period starts when the Plan is activated (clause 4.4), and each subsequent period starts when the previous period ends.

The Service initiates a charge no earlier than 3 (three) calendar days (72 hours) before the end of the paid billing period. If the charge fails (for example, because the account has insufficient funds or the bank declines the transaction), the Service retries no earlier than 20 hours after the previous attempt, with no more than 3 (three) attempts per billing period in total. An unsuccessful attempt does not shorten paid access or result in additional charges.

The new billing period is measured from the end of the previous paid period, regardless of the day on which the charge succeeds: the paid duration is not shortened, and a payment charged before the current period ends applies to the next billing period. If the charge succeeds after the paid period has already ended (within the remaining attempts), the new billing period is measured from the time of the charge; no fee is charged for the interval without access.

For the purposes of the Offer (including clauses 4.A.2-bis and 4.A.3), the date of the next charge means the end date of the current paid period, which is the latest date by which the charge will be initiated; that date and the amount of the next charge are displayed in the Account. To prevent a charge, the subscription must be disabled before the charge-initiation window begins (clause 4.A.4); a charge that has not been initiated before the subscription is disabled will not be made (clause 4.A.5), while a charge initiated before disabling and completed afterwards is governed by clause 5.3-bis.

If none of the attempts succeeds, access to the Plan remains available until the end of the paid period and ends when that period ends; the Service disables the subscription and notifies the User. The subscription may be resumed under clause 4.A.6 or, after the paid period ends, by making a new payment for a Plan.

For subscriptions taken out before the publication of Revision 4.1 of the Offer, the charge-initiation window in the second paragraph of this clause applies from the 8th (eighth) calendar day after publication; before that date, a charge for those subscriptions is initiated no earlier than 3 (three) hours before the end of the paid period.
