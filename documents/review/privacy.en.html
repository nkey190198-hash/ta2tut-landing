3. WHAT DATA WE PROCESS

3.1. Data received from the User during registration and use of the Service:

  • Telegram identifier (user_id): the numerical ID of the Telegram account
  • Username (if set): the public Telegram username
  • First and last name (if set in the Telegram profile): the display name
  • Telegram interface language

3.2. Data provided while using the Service:

  • Images: client photographs (with the client's consent), tattoo designs and photographs of completed work uploaded to the Service for processing
  • Message text: descriptions of ideas, requests for advice and feedback
  • Voice messages: for the feature that accepts ideas and requests by voice
  • Usage metadata: session times, selected features, quality ratings (👍/👎) and language preferences

3.3. Data received automatically:

  • Technical data: IP address (only when using the ta2tut.ru web interface), device type and Telegram client type
  • Cookies on ta2tut.ru (see Section 9)

3.4. Payment data:

When a payment is made, bank card details are transmitted directly to the payment service used for the payment: YooKassa (LLC NCO “YooMoney”, INN 7750005725) or Robokassa (LLC “Robokassa”, INN 5047063929). The Operator DOES NOT receive or store full bank card details. The Operator receives only a depersonalised transaction identifier and the payment amount from the payment service. When paying through the Faster Payments System (SBP), no card details are transmitted to the Operator at all.

4. PURPOSES OF PERSONAL DATA PROCESSING

The Operator processes personal data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Providing the Service: registration, activation of a Plan and performance of AI operations (stencil, try-on, design, retouching and video)
  • Billing and payments: processing payments through payment services (YooKassa and Robokassa), issuing receipts and recording transactions
  • Communication: sending notifications about operation status, changes to plans and maintenance, and responding to support requests
  • Analytics and quality improvement: depersonalised analysis of Service usage to improve AI models, fix errors and develop new features
  • Compliance with legal requirements: accounting and tax reporting

5. LEGAL GROUNDS FOR PROCESSING

Personal data is processed on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent of the data subject: given by the User upon registration with the Service (sending /start) and confirmed by each subsequent use of the features
  • Conclusion and performance of a contract: this Policy forms an integral part of the Public Offer (ta2tut.ru/oferta)
  • Performance of obligations imposed on the Operator by law (tax and accounting requirements)

6. HOW WE STORE AND PROTECT DATA

6.1. Storage locations:

  • Railway servers (USA/EU, iad data centres — Ashburn, Virginia, USA)
  • Backups on GitHub (USA)
  • Payment data: servers of the YooKassa and Robokassa payment services (Russia)
  • Image files: temporarily (up to 60 days) on Railway servers, after which they are automatically deleted
  • The ta2tut.ru website: Cloudflare Pages (distributed CDN)

6.2. Protection measures:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+)
  • Server access control: only the Operator's authorised employees have access
  • Regular backups
  • Logging of actions involving personal data
  • No disclosure of data to third parties except in the cases described in Section 7

6.3. Retention periods:

  • Images: up to 60 days from upload (video files: up to 5 days); voice messages: up to 7 days, after which they are automatically deleted
  • Session history: 7 days (for the “return to the conversation” feature)
  • Usage metadata (Telegram ID, selected features and ratings): until the User withdraws consent
  • Payment information (depersonalised transaction identifiers): 5 (five) years in accordance with tax legislation
  • Referral system records: until consent is withdrawn

8. USER RIGHTS

Under 152-ФЗ, the User has the right to:

  • Receive information about the processing of their personal data
  • Request correction, blocking or destruction of their personal data if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate or unlawfully obtained
  • Withdraw consent to processing at any time (in which case use of the Service will be terminated)
  • Receive a copy of their personal data in a structured form
  • Challenge the Operator's actions before Roskomnadzor (rkn.gov.ru) or a court

All requests must be sent to pishublock@gmail.com with the subject “Персональные данные” (“Personal data”), stating the Telegram username and/or Telegram ID. The response period is up to 30 (thirty) days from receipt.

How to withdraw consent and delete data:

  1. Send an email to pishublock@gmail.com with the subject “Удалить мои данные” (“Delete my data”).
  2. State your Telegram username and/or ID.
  3. Within 30 days, the Operator will delete all your data except data that must be retained by law (payment information).
  4. After deletion, you will be unable to use the Service. A paid Plan, if any, is refunded in proportion to the unused time.

9. COOKIES

The ta2tut.ru website uses a minimal set of technical cookies:

  • Session cookies: to display the page correctly

The website DOES NOT use:

  • Marketing or advertising cookies
  • Cross-site tracking systems
  • Third-party cookies (Google Analytics, Yandex Metrika, etc.)

The User can disable cookies in their browser settings; this will not affect the Service's operation in Telegram.

10.2. The Operator processes client images exclusively on the User's instructions (Article 6 of 152-ФЗ). The User acts as the operator commissioning the processing.

10.3. The Operator automatically deletes uploaded images within 60 days (video files within 5 days).

10.4. It is prohibited to upload images to the Service:

  • Containing intimate, extremist or unlawful content
